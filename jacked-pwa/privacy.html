Privacy Policy

Effective date: [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]

Jacked is a workout tracker. You log your lifts and cardio, see your progress, and compare with friends you add. This policy explains exactly what Jacked stores, where, who can see it, and how to get it deleted. It covers the Jacked iPhone app, the Jacked web app at jacked-trainer.netlify.app, and the Jacked watch apps, which all use the same account and the same data.

The short version: we only store what the app needs to work. No ads, no analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell or rent your data.

1. What we collect

Account

  • Your username (your public handle in Jacked, which friends use to add you) and your display name.
  • Your password. It is handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase Auth) and stored only as a one-way hash. We never see it in plain text.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to add one. It is optional and used only as another way to sign in. We do not send marketing email.

Your training data

  • Workouts: date, name, exercises, sets, weights, reps, distances, durations and paces, personal records, routines, custom exercises you create, and your settings.
  • In-progress workout: while a workout is running it is synced so your phone and watch can show the same session.
  • Heart rate and calories, only if you use a Jacked watch app. The watch records them during a workout and they are saved with that workout.

Body metrics (optional)

  • Body weight (and its history, if you log it over time), birthday, and sex (male/female). These are used only to calculate strength rankings and badges on your own Metrics screen. You can leave them blank.

Profile photo (optional)

  • If you upload one, it is stored so friends can see it.

Friends

  • Who you have sent friend requests to, received them from, and accepted.

Feedback

  • Anything you type into the in-app Feedback box, together with your username so we can follow up.

Security data

  • When someone tries to sign in, the IP address the attempt came from is kept in a rate-limit counter for a short window so repeated guessing can be slowed down. It is not linked to your account.

What we do not collect: your location, contacts, address book, browsing history, advertising identifiers, payment information, or any usage analytics. Jacked has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software in it.

2. Why we use it

Only to run the app for you:

  • keep you signed in and protect your account;
  • save your workouts to the cloud so they survive a lost or replaced phone and stay in sync between your phone, the web app and your watch;
  • show your progress, personal records, badges and rankings;
  • let the friends you choose see your training and compare on leaderboards;
  • read and act on feedback you send.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or anything unrelated to the app.

3. Who can see what

Only you: your full workout history, body weight, birthday, sex, email address, settings, and your backup copies.

Friends you have accepted can see: your display name, username and photo; your total volume lifted, number of workouts, personal record count, streak, consistency and badges; which days you trained; and a summary of your workouts from roughly the last 30 days (workout name and date, each exercise, and the weight and reps of each set, or distance and pace for cardio). Friends never see your body weight, birthday, sex or email.

Any signed-in Jacked user can look up a username and see that account's username and profile photo, so friend requests work. Profile photos are stored in a public storage folder, so anyone who has a photo's exact web address can open it. Do not upload a photo you would not want to be public.

Nobody else. Access is enforced on the server by database rules, not just hidden in the app.

4. Service providers that handle your data

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone for their own purposes. These companies process it for us so the app can run:

  • Supabase, Inc. hosts the Jacked database, sign-in system and file storage (project qehlrwnumrjktujidabz, running on Amazon Web Services infrastructure). Everything listed in section 1 is stored there. Supabase also keeps short-lived technical logs of requests (including IP addresses) for operating and securing its service.
  • Netlify, Inc. hosts the Jacked web app's files. Like any web host it receives your IP address and browser details when you load the web app.
  • Web app only: the web app loads its fonts from Google Fonts (Google LLC) and one code library from the jsDelivr network. Your browser contacts them directly when the web app loads, which gives them your IP address and browser details. No account or workout data is sent to them. The iPhone app does not use either.
  • Apple distributes the iPhone app through the App Store and TestFlight under Apple's own privacy policy.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, for example a valid court order.

5. Your data on your device

The app also keeps a copy of your data on your device so it works offline and loads quickly. Signing out clears it from that device. You can export a full copy of your data at any time from Profile, Data & Backup, Export.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account, workouts and profile: for as long as you have an account.
  • Backup restore points: Jacked keeps up to 14 daily restore points of your backup so lost data can be recovered after a sync problem. Older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Feedback: until it has been dealt with.
  • Sign-in rate-limit counters: overwritten as new attempts come in; they are not tied to an account.
  • When your account is deleted, everything above that belongs to it is deleted too, including restore points and your photo.

7. Deleting your account and your rights

You can ask us to delete your account and all of its data at any time by emailing [[CONTACT_EMAIL]] from the email on your account, or by sending a request through the in-app Feedback box. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data (or export it yourself, see section 5), correct it (most of it you can edit in the app), or ask any question about how it is handled. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA; write to us and we will honour them.

8. Security

Data travels only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are stored hashed. Database rules make sure only you (and, for the parts listed in section 3, your accepted friends) can read your data, and repeated sign-in guessing is rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we store to the minimum the app needs.

9. Children

Jacked is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will update it here and change the effective date at the top. If a change affects how your existing data is used, we will tell you in the app first.
